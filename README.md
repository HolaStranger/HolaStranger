## 🔮 About Me

</div>

I'm a Software Engineering graduate from **Universiti Sains Malaysia** who builds systems where automation, AI, and full stack engineering meet, designed to run in production, not just demos.

My work centers on three things: **enterprise automation** that removes manual work from real business teams, **AI integrated platforms** that apply LLMs to solve concrete operational problems, and **full stack engineering** that ties it all together into something people actually use. During my internship at a global BPO, I shipped a suite of production automation systems used daily across HR, Finance, and Operations. On the AI side, I've architected event driven platforms with LLM pipelines for classification and decision support, applied machine learning to real datasets, and built multilingual conversational systems supporting candidate screening across multiple languages.

I care about clean architecture, scalable design, and systems that hold up under real usage, not just code that runs once in a demo.
